02 / THE HUMAN BEHIND THE PIXELS

A creative brain.
A practical pair of hands.

I’m Charlie, from South Devon. I graduated in Computer Games Design with First Class Honours and received the TIGA Graduate of the Year award for Artist in 2023.

Since then, I’ve worked on game-ready 3D assets, made coffee in busy cafés, helped at live events and kept exploring how things work. I’m also building a path towards IT and cybersecurity, with a Level 2 qualification in the Principles of Cyber Security.

Outside work? Music, vintage cameras, retro tech and finding something interesting around the next corner. Ideally with a camera in hand.
