## Existing portfolio work

`assets/stray-robot.jpg` is the existing preview of Charlie Woodhead's [Stray-inspired original robot character on Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/stray-robot-fan-art-original-character-ce0619a05bbd4f40b1f296c1d78efd0f). The case-study process and model facts come from that project's public description and metadata: final-year university work, low-poly modelling in 3ds Max, high-poly sculpting in ZBrush, texturing in Substance Painter, published 12 May 2023, 48,269 triangles. The interactive viewer embeds that same model.

The existing Gremloblin and Pyroow previews retain their original portfolio links and on-page fan-art/concept credits. Pyroow's concept is credited to Hicham Habchi. The homepage portrait and existing game assets are retained from the original repository.
